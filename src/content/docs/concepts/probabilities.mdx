"70 % chance of snow" is ambiguous until you say: snow of how much, where,
and over what period. Most people misread a bare percentage. Every Ourea
probability names its **reference class**, and your interface should too.

## The four parts

| Part | In a `ProbabilityResult` | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Event and threshold** | `variable`, `op`, `threshold` (in the request's units) | snowfall ≥ 6 in |
| **Place** | the forecast's `location` (or `point`) and its elevation | Mt. Hood Meadows, 1,661 m |
| **Window** | `window_start_hours`, `window_end_hours`, `from`, `to` (`to` exclusive) | the next 24 h |
| **How it was counted** | `aggregation`, `members` | the window total, 131 members |

## How the server counts

- Members are pooled across models, one vote each.
- An hour is in window `A-B h` when its `valid_time` is in
  [first + A h, first + B h), where "first" is the first served hour.
- `any`: some hour of the window meets the threshold. `all`: every hour does.
  `total`: the window total meets it (precipitation and snowfall, where totals
  are counted as for [window totals](/features/window-totals/)).
- `probability` is the share of voting members that meet it, to 0.001.
  `members` is how many voted: for `total`, the members whose values cover the
  whole window.
- `null` means no answer: the served series does not reach the window's end.
  It is never 0.

## How to word it

Number first, then the event, threshold, place and window:

> **70 %** chance of **at least 6 in of snow** at **Mt. Hood Meadows summit**,
> **6 pm Friday to 6 am Saturday**.

- Phrase the event the way the decision is made ("at least 6 in" for a powder
  alert); don't headline the complement.
- If you add a word ("likely"), take it from one fixed word-to-number table,
  always shown with the number.
- Show the window in the location's own timezone (`location.timezone`).

## Built-in probabilities

The hourly `ensemble.precipitation.probability` is P(at least a 0.1 mm
trace); the daily `precipitation_probability` is P(day total ≥ 2.5 mm) and
`precipitation_occurrence_probability` P(≥ 1.0 mm). Each response's `thresholds`
block names these events in your units. They are different events: a day can
have a 60 % chance of any precipitation and a 30 % chance of a meaningful
amount.

Ask for your own events with [`prob=`](/features/probabilities/).
