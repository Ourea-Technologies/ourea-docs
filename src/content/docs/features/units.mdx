`units=` sets the unit system of every value in the response:

| `units` | Temperature | Precipitation, snowfall | Wind | Elevation, snow level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `metric` (default) | °C | mm | m/s | m |
| `imperial` | °F | in | mph | ft |
| `kelvin` | K | mm | m/s | m |

- **The server converts; your code doesn't.** To show another unit system,
  ask for it. The SDKs never convert response values.
- **The body says which.** Every forecast has `units`, so a cached or shared
  body says whether `32.0` is °F or °C.
- **Thresholds are in the request's units.** `prob=snowfall:ge:6.000:24h`
  means 6 in only with `units=imperial`; on a metric request write
  `152.40`. The SDKs convert a threshold you give with an explicit unit, with
  exact factors (1 in = 25.4 mm, 1 ft = 0.3048 m, 1 mph = 0.44704 m/s).
- **Some fields are fixed.** `elevation_m` is always metres, `distance_km`
  always kilometres; `elevation_m` on `/forecast` is sent in metres whatever
  `units` is. Pressure is hPa (metric, kelvin) or inHg (imperial); relative
  humidity and cloud cover are percent; CAPE is J/kg.
- **Defaults are omitted.** Don't send `units=metric`: it is the default, and
  the canonical URL leaves it out.
- **Cap'n Proto** bodies are in the request's units too, except search, which
  is metric.
