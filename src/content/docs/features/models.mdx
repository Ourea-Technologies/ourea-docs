## The blend

Each forecast hour is blended from up to five ensembles: ECMWF AIFS-ENS
(`aifs`) and IFS-ENS (`ifs_ens`), and NOAA AIGEFS (`aigefs`), GEFS (`gefs`)
and RRFS (`rrfs`). The blend is **per-model-latest**: every model contributes
its freshest complete cycle within its staleness cap (`aifs`, `aigefs` and
`gefs` 30 h, `ifs_ens` 36 h, `rrfs` 14 h), aligned by valid time, so the
models are generally at different inits.

`model_run` in every forecast says what was blended:

- `initialized_at`: the reference init, the newest contributing model's
  cycle. Every `lead_time_hours` counts from it.
- `total_members`.
- `models[]`: one `{id, cycle, members}` per contributing model, in the order
  `aifs`, `aigefs`, `gefs`, `ifs_ens`, `rrfs`; a model added later follows. A
  model that contributed nothing is absent. Treat unknown ids as additive.

Which models feed which hours, from the same response: the global ensembles
publish every 6 hours, RRFS every hour out to 84 h.

## Per-model breakdowns (Pro)

`models=true` adds `model_means[]` to every hour: each model's own statistics
(`{id, temperature, dewpoint, precipitation, wind_speed, wind_direction,
snow_level, slr, snowfall}`), with `interpolated: true` where a 6-hourly model
was interpolated between its native steps. A Free or anonymous caller gets
`403 PRO_REQUIRED`.

## Runs

`GET /api/v1/runs` lists the cycles `run=` can pin (init times at least two
models completed, newest first) and, in `served`, what a forecast serves now.
The two differ by design: `runs[0]` can be 12Z while forecasts say 18Z,
because one model's 18Z cycle is in and a second has not finished it. The
route works without credentials.

A pinned run (`run=<RFC 3339 init>`, Pro) serves that historical cycle, every
model at that init, with the hourly series from its init.
