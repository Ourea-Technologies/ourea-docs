## `/current`

`GET /api/v1/locations/{state}/{slug}/current` works without credentials.

- `current`: "right now" as one flat object, read from the served hour nearest
  the request time: `temperature`, `feels_like`, `condition`,
  `condition_text`, `condition_icon`, `wind_speed`, `wind_gust`,
  `wind_direction`, `relative_humidity`, `dewpoint`, `precip_probability`,
  and `source` (`nowcast` when a live observation carried weight, `model` when
  none did), plus the anchoring `station` when there was one. It is `null` when
  no cycle is servable.
- `sensors[]`: each nearby sensor's latest readings (within 25 km, from the
  last 3 hours), in your units under plain names: `temperature`, `dewpoint`,
  `wind_speed`, `wind_gust`, `pressure`, `precipitation`, `visibility`,
  `snow_depth`, `swe`, `swe_percent_of_median`, `pavement_temp` and more,
  each present only where the sensor takes it. With `station_id`, `name`,
  `source` (the network) and `observation_time`.
- `alerts[]`, `alerts_as_of`: the active alerts, also on `/alerts`.
- `include=nowcast_hours` adds the live forecast band
  ([Nowcast](/features/nowcast/)).
- `sources` and `exclude_sources` choose observation networks (`snotel`,
  `raws`, `nws`, `ski_resort` and more).

A forecast's `nearest_sensors[]` lists the nearby sensors ranked by an
elevation-weighted distance (`effective_distance_km` = `distance_km` +
elevation difference in metres / 100).

## Alerts

`GET /api/v1/locations/{state}/{slug}/alerts` returns the active NWS watches,
warnings and advisories for the location, matched by NWS zone and, for
convective products, by warning polygon: `id`, `event`, `severity`,
`urgency`, `headline`, `onset`, `expires`, `ends`. Alerts are ingested about
every 2 minutes and served `Cache-Control: private, no-cache` with an `ETag`:
safety data never sits in a shared cache, and a poll of unchanged alerts is a
`304` with no body.

## At coordinates (Pro)

`/api/v1/current?lat&lon` and `/api/v1/alerts?lat&lon` return the same bodies
for any point, resolved exactly as [`/forecast`](/features/places/#coordinates-pro)
resolves it. Free and anonymous callers get `403 PRO_REQUIRED`.

## Storm reports

`GET /api/v1/storm-reports` lists verified storm "report cards", one per
completed event where both forecast and observed totals have landed: forecast
and observed totals, the best-performing model and its lead time, a composite
skill score and a headline. It works without credentials.
