`members=true` adds each member's values to every hour of the series. One
request carries every model's members for every hour:

<Example task="plume" op="getLocationForecast" />

- Each `timeseries[]` hour has `members`: one `{id, members}` entry per model
  with members at that hour, in `model_run.models` order. A member is
  identified by `(model, member_id)`; `member_id` is stable across the hours
  of a response and across cycles.
- A member's `precipitation` and `snowfall` are totals over its
  `precipitation_hours` ending at `valid_time`: 1 for an hourly model, 6 for a
  6-hourly one, which has values at every 6th hour only.
- Every member carries its running totals, `cumulative_precipitation` and
  `cumulative_snowfall`, over the served window, and the synoptic hours carry
  their distribution in `members_cumulative` (computed from the members'
  totals, never by summing hourly percentiles). Draw these served fields;
  compute nothing.
- Members are at `location.elevation_m`, and on each served
  `elevation_forecasts[]` entry at its own elevation.
- A `null` member value means that member published nothing at that hour;
  a measured `0.0` is a dry member. They are different claims.

## Plume view and full view

`members_view` says which you got:

| | `plume` (anonymous and Free) | `full` (Pro) |
|---|---|---|
| Variables | temperature, precipitation (with `precipitation_hours`), snow level, snowfall | every member variable, including wind speed, wind direction and SLR |

In the plume view a `null` wind or SLR is withheld by the view, not missing.

## Caching a plume

The request above is the cacheable base layer: `nowcast=false` makes it
deterministic for the life of the served cycle, so an anonymous caller gets a
shared-cache TTL until the next expected model cycle, under a strong ETag
(`304` on `If-None-Match`). Send its parameters in alphabetical order, as
shown: the edge cache keys on the exact URL. Ask for the whole horizon
(`max_hours=240`, Pro `360`). See [Caching and ETags](/features/caching/).

<Aside>
The headline total is the blend's mean, served as `snow_outlook` and the
hourly `cumulative_*` fields, not the members' mean.
</Aside>
