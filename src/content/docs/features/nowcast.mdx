A forecast has two layers.

- **Layer B, the nowcast** (`nowcast=true`, the default): hours 0 to 6 are
  blended with live observations, today's daily range is clamped to what was
  observed, and `nearest_sensors`, gap flow and the overnight snow outlook are
  filled in. In those hours `precipitation` mixes the forecast with what the
  MRMS radar rain rate predicts for each hour, at full weight for the hour of
  the scan, falling to about half an hour later and an eighth by +6 h. The
  hour in progress and the hour just ended read what the radar has already
  measured, where MRMS has published it. `nowcast_applied: true`.
- **Layer A, the base forecast** (`nowcast=false`): the pure model forecast,
  deterministic for the life of the served cycle, with no live layers
  (`nowcast_applied: false`, `nearest_sensors` empty).

If a live source does not answer in time, the forecast is served without it
and names it in `nowcast_unavailable` (`observation`, `radar`, `cloud`,
`nearest_sensors`): a request never waits on a slow observation read.

## Base plus overlay

For clients that cache, fetch the base forecast once per cycle and overlay the
live band from `/current?include=nowcast_hours`:

- `nowcast_hours` holds the observation-blended hours with valid times from
  one hour before `as_of` to ten hours after, shaped like the forecast's
  `timeseries` points. **For valid times it covers, the band wins** over the
  cached base.
- `model_run_initialized_at` names the cycle the band was read from: when it
  changes, your cached base is from an older cycle, so refetch it.
- The base layer's anonymous TTL runs to the next expected model cycle; the
  merged default's is 60 s and `/current`'s 30 s. See
  [Caching and ETags](/features/caching/).

The default stays `nowcast=true`, permanently.
