## Quantiles

Every forecast statistic is one shape, `Quantiles`: `mean`, `p10`, `p25`,
`p50`, `p75`, `p90`, and `min`/`max` where the statistic has them, in the
request's units. Percentiles are over the ensemble members. Read
[What p10, p50 and p90 mean](/concepts/quantiles/) before you display them.

- Show a range as p10 to p90, with p50 as "most likely".
- For precipitation and snowfall, prefer `p50` over `mean`: the mean is
  inflated by a few wet members and over-predicts at a point.
- Never add quantiles across hours. Ask for a
  [window total](/features/window-totals/) instead
  ([why](/concepts/adding-quantiles/)).

## Probabilities with `prob=`

Ask for the chance of an event and the server computes it from every member:

```text
prob=<variable>:<ge|le>:<threshold>:<window>[:<aggregation>]
```

<Example task="chance-of-snow" op="getLocationForecast" />

| Part | Values |
|---|---|
| `variable` | `temperature`, `wind_speed`, `snow_level`, `precipitation`, `snowfall` |
| `op` | `ge` (at least) or `le` (at most) |
| `threshold` | A number in the request's units, at the variable's served precision: 3 decimals for imperial precipitation and snowfall (`6.000`), 2 for metric (`152.40`), 1 for temperature and wind, 0 for snow level |
| `window` | `<N>h` (hours 0 to N from the first served hour) or `<A>-<B>h`. `0-24h` is written `24h`. Up to 360 h |
| `aggregation` | `total` for precipitation and snowfall (the window total; the only one allowed for them). `any` (the default: some hour meets it) or `all` (every hour) for the others. Omit the default |

Each answer in `probabilities[]` is a `ProbabilityResult` that carries its own
reference class: `variable`, `op`, `threshold`, `window_start_hours`,
`window_end_hours`, `from`, `to`, `aggregation`, `members` (how many members
voted) and `probability` (0 to 1). The answers come back in the order the
terms were sent.

`probability` is `null` when the served series does not reach the window's
end: ask a larger `max_hours` to cover it (the example sends `max_hours=72`
for its 72 h term). Treat `null` as "no answer", never as 0.

### The canonical form

The edge cache keys on the exact URL, so send the canonical spelling: terms
de-duplicated and sorted by variable, then op (`ge` before `le`), then window
(start, then end), then aggregation, then threshold; joined with a literal
`,`, with literal `:` inside each term. The SDKs do this for you and map
the answers back to the order you asked in.

<Aside>
Read [What a probability refers to](/concepts/probabilities/) before you show
one: a probability means nothing without its event, threshold, place and
window.
</Aside>

## Built-in probabilities

Some probabilities are served without asking: each hour's
`ensemble.precipitation.probability` and `ensemble.snowfall.probability`, and
each day's `precip_probability`, `precip_occurrence_probability` and
`snow_probability`. Their thresholds are echoed in the response's
`thresholds` block. See [Variables](/features/variables/).
