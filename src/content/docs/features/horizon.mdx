- **The series starts now.** `forecast.timeseries` begins at the hour
  containing the request time and runs `max_hours` past it: `max_hours=48` is
  the current hour plus the next 48, 49 points. `lead_time_hours` still counts
  from `model_run.initialized_at`.
- **`max_hours`** defaults to 24 (6 to 360) and is clamped to the tier's
  outlook: Free 240 h, Pro 360 h. The series ends where the model run ends
  (lead 360), so near the cap fewer hours come back.
- **`max_days`** sets the number of `daily` rows, independent of
  `max_hours`, from the local date of forecast hour 1. Default: the tier's full
  horizon (Free 10, Pro 15).
- **Clamped, not refused.** The response's `applied` block says what was
  served (`max_hours`, `max_days`) and lists in `clamped` the options the
  server changed.
- **`include_past=true`** serves the hourly series from the model run's init,
  the hours already past included, for research and verification. A pinned
  `run` is always served from its init.
- **`hourly=false`** serves the daily rows alone; `daily=false` drops them.
  Both false is a `400`.

The `daily` rows cover whole local days either way, and `snow_outlook`'s
windows open at the first served hour.
