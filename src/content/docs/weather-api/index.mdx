[Ourea](https://oureawx.com) is a weather API built around uncertainty. Each
forecast hour for the contiguous United States is blended from up to five
ensembles (ECMWF AIFS-ENS and IFS-ENS, NOAA AIGEFS, GEFS and RRFS), calibrated,
and served with its full distribution: quantiles for every variable,
probabilities for the events you name, and storm-total ranges computed from
each member's own total.

## What you get

- **Forecasts by place.** Ski areas, mountain passes, trailheads, summits,
  towns and airports by state and slug; any point by latitude and longitude
  with Pro. [Places](/features/places/).
- **Probabilities on request.** `prob=snowfall:ge:6.000:24h` returns the share
  of members with at least 6 in in 24 h, with the window and member count.
  [Probabilities](/features/probabilities/).
- **Window totals.** The range of a storm total, done right.
  [Window totals](/features/window-totals/).
- **Plumes.** Every member, every hour. [Plumes](/features/plumes/).
- **Live layers.** The first hours blend station observations, MRMS radar and
  GOES cloud; `/current` and NWS alerts. [Nowcast](/features/nowcast/).
- **Two formats.** JSON, or the same values as Cap'n Proto.
  [JSON or Cap'n Proto?](/json-or-capnp/)
- **Cache-friendly.** Canonical URLs, strong ETags, `304`s.
  [Caching](/features/caching/).

## Free, and what needs Pro

Anonymous requests and Free keys are served at the Free tier: named-location
forecasts to 10 days (240 h) from the latest run, current conditions and
alerts for named locations, search, nearby and the catalog. Forecasts,
current conditions and alerts **at coordinates**, per-model data, pinned
runs and the 15-day horizon need Pro. [The free tier in full](/weather-api/free/).
