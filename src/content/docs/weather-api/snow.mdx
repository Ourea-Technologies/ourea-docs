Snow is where a single number misleads most: timing, snow level and
snow-to-liquid ratio all vary member to member. Ourea serves snow as a
distribution and lets you ask the questions skiers and road crews actually
ask.

## "Will we get 6 inches?"

<Example task="chance-of-snow" op="getLocationForecast" />

Each answer is the share of ensemble members whose own snowfall total over the
window reaches the threshold, with the window and member count.
[Probabilities](/features/probabilities/).

## "How much, at most and at least?"

The storm-total range, from each member's own total:

<Example task="storm-total" op="getLocationForecast" />

Show it as "most likely p50, at least p10, could reach p90". Never sum hourly
quantiles ([why](/concepts/adding-quantiles/)).

## Every snow field

- Hourly `snowfall` at the location's elevation, with the chance of measurable
  snow; `slr` (snow-to-liquid ratio); `snow_level`; `freezing_level`;
  `precip_type` with `snow_probability`.
- Daily `total_snowfall`, `snow_probability` (P(≥ 2.5 mm)),
  `total_snowfall_if_wet`, `snow_level`, and `storm_orientation`
  (how the snow ratio changes through a storm).
- `snow_outlook`: next 24 h, 48 h and 7 days, plus overnight (4 pm to 9 am),
  with ranges.
- Plumes: every member's running snowfall total.

[Variables](/features/variables/) lists them all. For ski areas, ask for the
base, mid and summit with `elevations=all`
([Elevation](/concepts/elevation/)).
