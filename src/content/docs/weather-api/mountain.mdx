Mountain weather changes with every few hundred metres. Ourea computes each
forecast at a stated elevation and serves its uncertainty, for the places
mountain users go.

## Mountain places in the catalog

The catalog includes ski areas, summits, mountain passes, trailheads,
backcountry areas, fire lookouts, volcanoes and climbing areas, each with
its elevation and timezone. Search by type:

<Example task="search" op="searchLocations" />

## Forecasts at each elevation

Ski areas have named base, mid and summit points; ask for all of them with
`elevations=all`, each a full forecast at its own elevation:

<Example task="elevations" op="getLocationForecast" />

With Pro, forecast any point and pass `elevation_m` for a summit, a lift top
or a ridge. [Elevation](/concepts/elevation/).

## What mountain users need

- **Snow level and freezing level**, hour by hour.
- **Wind and gusts**, with `gust_notable` for lift and ridge decisions.
- **Inversions** and cold pools, flagged when detected.
- **Snow totals** with ranges, and probabilities over your window.
- **Nearby sensors**, including SNOTEL snow depth and SWE, on `/current`.
- **NWS alerts** for the zone.
