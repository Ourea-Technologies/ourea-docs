Ourea's Free tier costs nothing and needs no signup. It is a real forecast,
not a demo: the same calibrated ensemble blend Pro gets, with the same
quantiles and the same server-side probabilities, for every named location in
the catalog.

## Free, in one sentence

Requests without a key, and requests with a Free key, get **forecasts for
named locations** (ski areas, passes, trailheads, summits, towns, airports and
more) to a **10-day (240 h) horizon** from the **latest model run**, with
probabilities, window totals and the plume view of the ensemble members, plus
**current conditions and NWS alerts** for those locations, **search**,
**nearby** and the **catalog**.

What Free does **not** include, plainly: forecasts, current conditions and
alerts **at arbitrary coordinates** (`/api/v1/forecast?lat&lon` is Pro), the
**15-day** horizon, **per-model** breakdowns, **pinned historical runs**,
and wind and snow-ratio values in the member plumes. A Free caller asking for
one of those gets `403 PRO_REQUIRED`, never a silently different answer.

## Is a free key worth getting?

The Free tier is the same with or without a key. A key gives you your own
budget instead of your network address's, and it is the step before Pro.
Self-serve keys open at launch; see [Get an API key](/getting-started/#get-an-api-key).

## When you need Pro

Coordinates (any point, with elevation), 15 days, per-model data, historical
runs, full member plumes and a larger budget: see
[oureawx.com/pricing](https://oureawx.com/pricing).
