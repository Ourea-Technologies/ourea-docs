"30 % chance of rain" means little until you know of how much, where and
when. Ourea serves precipitation probabilities with their event spelled out,
and lets you ask for your own.

## Served on every forecast

| Field | The event |
|---|---|
| hourly `ensemble.precipitation.probability` | at least a trace (0.1 mm) in the hour |
| daily `precip_occurrence_probability` | at least 1.0 mm in the local day: "will it precipitate at all" |
| daily `precip_probability` | at least 2.5 mm in the day: a meaningful amount |
| daily `snow_probability` | at least 2.5 mm of snowfall in the day |

Each response's `thresholds` block echoes those thresholds in your units.
`precip_display` is the server's chosen daily headline (none, rain, snow, mix
or freezing rain, with its chance and amount), so every client words it the
same way.

## Ask for your own

Any amount, any window up to 360 h:

```text
prob=precipitation:ge:0.500:48h&units=imperial
```

The server counts the share of members whose own total over the window
reaches the threshold, and tells you how many voted.

## Amounts with their range

Pair the chance with the amount if wet (`total_precip_if_wet`), and the
window total's range with `totals=precipitation:24h`.
